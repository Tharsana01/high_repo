01 — About

Beyond the test case

A quality engineer's job doesn't end at "pass" or "fail" — it's about designing confidence into a system before it ever reaches a customer.

I'm an SDET who moved from writing test cases to building the systems that write, run, and reason about them.

Over three years at Comcast, I've built automation frameworks for Sky Germany's billing and CRM platforms — covering everything from Salesforce order journeys to Oracle BRM pro-ration logic — and embedded quality gates directly into CI/CD pipelines so issues surface before release, not after.

More recently, that work has extended into AI-powered QA: agentic test orchestration with Claude and MCP, and internal tools that turn documentation and reports into something a teammate can just ask a question about.
